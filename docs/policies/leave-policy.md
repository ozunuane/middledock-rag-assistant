# MiddleDock Leave & Time-Off Policy

Effective: January 2026

## Paid Time Off (PTO)

- All full-time employees receive 20 days PTO per year
- PTO accrues at 1.67 days per month
- Maximum carryover: 5 days into the next calendar year
- Unused PTO beyond carryover is forfeited on January 1st
- PTO requests must be submitted at least 5 business days in advance
- Requests of 5+ consecutive days require 2 weeks notice

## Sick Leave

- 10 days paid sick leave per year
- Sick leave does not roll over
- A doctor's note is required for absences of 3+ consecutive days
- Sick leave may be used for personal illness, family member illness, or medical appointments
- Mental health days are included under sick leave

## Parental Leave

- Primary caregiver: 16 weeks fully paid
- Secondary caregiver: 6 weeks fully paid
- Applies to birth, adoption, and foster placement
- Leave must begin within 12 months of the qualifying event
- Employees may extend leave with unpaid time (up to 26 weeks total) with manager approval

## Bereavement

- Immediate family (spouse, child, parent, sibling): 5 days paid
- Extended family (grandparent, in-law, aunt/uncle): 3 days paid
- Close friend: 1 day paid
- Additional unpaid time may be requested through HR

## Public Holidays

MiddleDock observes 10 public holidays per year (US calendar). Employees in other countries follow their local public holiday calendar. If a public holiday falls on a weekend, the following Monday is observed.

## Sabbatical

- Employees with 5+ years of service are eligible for a 4-week paid sabbatical
- Sabbatical must be taken in a single continuous block
- One sabbatical per 5-year period
- Request must be submitted 3 months in advance

## Jury Duty & Voting

- Paid leave for jury duty (unlimited duration)
- Up to 2 hours paid leave for voting on election days
