# MiddleDock Remote Work Policy

Effective: January 2026
Last Updated: October 2026

## Eligibility

All full-time employees who have completed their 90-day probation period are eligible for remote work. Contract employees must receive written approval from their department head.

## Work Schedule

- Core hours: 10:00 AM - 3:00 PM (employee's local timezone)
- Employees must be available on Slack during core hours
- Flexible scheduling is permitted outside core hours
- Maximum 3 days remote per week for hybrid roles
- Fully remote roles have no in-office requirement

## Equipment

- MiddleDock provides a laptop, monitor, and keyboard for all remote workers
- Equipment must be returned within 14 days of termination
- Employees are responsible for their own internet connection (minimum 50 Mbps)
- A monthly stipend of $75 is provided for internet and utilities

## Security

- All work must be performed on company-issued devices
- VPN must be active when accessing internal systems
- Screen locks must engage after 5 minutes of inactivity
- Work from public WiFi is prohibited without VPN
- Sensitive documents must not be printed at home

## International Remote Work

- Working from outside your country of employment requires HR approval at least 30 days in advance
- Maximum 30 calendar days per year of international remote work
- Tax implications are the employee's responsibility
- Some countries are restricted due to data sovereignty requirements (see Restricted Countries List)

## Performance

- Remote employees are held to the same performance standards as in-office employees
- Managers conduct monthly check-ins via video call
- If performance declines, remote privileges may be revoked with 2 weeks notice
