# MiddleDock Code of Conduct

Effective: January 2026

## Our Values

MiddleDock is a logistics technology company connecting small-to-medium businesses with affordable shipping and warehousing solutions. We operate in 12 countries and employ 450 people.

Our values: Ship Fast, Stay Honest, Help Each Other, Think Long-Term.

## Workplace Behavior

- Treat all colleagues, customers, and partners with respect
- Harassment, discrimination, and bullying are zero-tolerance offenses
- Report concerns to your manager, HR, or the anonymous ethics hotline
- Retaliation against anyone reporting a concern is prohibited
- All complaints are investigated within 5 business days

## Communication Standards

- Default to public Slack channels over DMs for work discussions
- Assume positive intent in written communication
- Video on for meetings unless bandwidth is an issue
- No meetings on Fridays (Focus Friday policy)
- Respond to Slack messages within 4 hours during business hours

## Conflicts of Interest

- Disclose any financial interest in a competitor, vendor, or customer
- Do not accept gifts worth more than $50 from vendors
- Side employment is permitted if it doesn't conflict with MiddleDock work — disclose to HR
- Romantic relationships between direct reports must be disclosed to HR

## Intellectual Property

- All work created during employment belongs to MiddleDock
- Open-source contributions during work hours require manager approval
- Do not share proprietary information on social media, forums, or conferences without approval
- NDAs are signed by all employees and contractors at onboarding

## Disciplinary Process

1. **Verbal warning** — documented in HR system
2. **Written warning** — includes improvement plan with 30-day timeline
3. **Final warning** — includes PIP with 60-day timeline
4. **Termination** — if no improvement after PIP

Severe violations (theft, harassment, data breach) may result in immediate termination.
