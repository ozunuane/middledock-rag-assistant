# MiddleDock Expense & Reimbursement Policy

Effective: January 2026

## General Rules

- All expenses must be submitted within 30 days of incurrence
- Receipts are required for any expense over $25
- Expenses must be submitted through the Expensify portal
- Reimbursements are processed on the 15th of each month
- Pre-approval is required for any single expense over $500

## Travel

### Flights
- Economy class for flights under 6 hours
- Premium economy permitted for flights over 6 hours
- Business class requires VP approval
- Book at least 14 days in advance when possible
- Use the corporate travel portal (Navan) for all bookings

### Hotels
- Maximum nightly rate: $200 (domestic), $250 (international)
- Exceptions for high-cost cities (NYC, SF, London, Tokyo): $350/night
- Extended stays (5+ nights): contact procurement for corporate rates

### Meals
- Daily meal allowance: $75 (domestic), $100 (international)
- Team dinners: $50 per person including drinks
- Client entertainment: $100 per person, requires manager pre-approval
- Alcohol is reimbursable only with client entertainment

### Ground Transportation
- Rideshare (Uber/Lyft) is preferred over rental cars for single-day travel
- Rental cars require pre-approval and must include insurance
- Mileage reimbursement for personal vehicles: $0.67/mile

## Software & Subscriptions

- Individual subscriptions under $30/month: manager approval via Slack
- Subscriptions over $30/month: submitted through IT procurement
- Annual subscriptions over $500: requires department head approval
- All SaaS tools must be vetted by IT security before purchase

## Conference & Training

- Annual learning budget: $2,000 per employee
- Conference attendance requires manager approval 30 days in advance
- Conference travel follows standard travel policy
- Online courses and certifications are encouraged and covered under learning budget

## Non-Reimbursable Expenses

- Personal phone bills (unless on-call rotation)
- Home office furniture (covered under remote work stipend instead)
- Parking tickets or traffic violations
- Personal travel extensions added to business trips
- First-class flights under any circumstances
